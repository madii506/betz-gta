What are the odds?

The crowd. The split shows every open call on that market right now. No house, no made-up numbers: zero calls means an empty bar.

When is a call graded?

At the first check after its 24 hours are up. Every visit to the board runs the grader, so it lands within minutes on a busy day.

How many calls can I make?

One open call per market per coin, up to 40 open at once. When a call is graded you can call that market again.

What does signing do?

It proves the call came from your wallet. The message says the coin, the market, your side and the time. It can't move funds.
